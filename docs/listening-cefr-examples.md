# Listening Activity CEFR Examples

Same transcript excerpt:

> Many companies now allow remote work, but managers still worry about trust, communication, and long-term team culture. Some employees feel more focused at home, while others miss quick feedback and informal conversations.

## B1 Output Direction

True / False focuses on explicit comprehension:

- The speaker talks about remote work.
- Some managers worry about trust and communication.
- All employees feel more focused at home.

Gap Fill prefers frequent, practical vocabulary:

- Many companies now allow ______.
- Managers worry about ______ and communication.

Word bank:

- remote work
- trust
- communication
- focused
- feedback

Discussion questions use minimal inference:

- What problem does the speaker mention?
- Do you prefer working at home or in an office? Why?

## B2 Output Direction

True / False adds moderate inference and workplace language:

- The speaker suggests remote work creates both benefits and concerns.
- Communication is presented as a workplace challenge.
- The speaker says office work is always better than remote work.

Gap Fill prefers collocations and workplace vocabulary:

- Managers worry about long-term ______.
- Employees may miss quick ______.
- Remote work can affect informal ______.

Word bank:

- team culture
- quick feedback
- informal conversations
- workplace expectations
- balanced approach

Discussion questions invite reasons and trade-offs:

- What trade-off does the speaker suggest between focus and team culture?
- How could a company reduce communication problems in remote work?

## C1 Output Direction

True / False tests nuance, implicit ideas, and argument structure:

- The speaker frames remote work as a tension between autonomy and cohesion.
- The concern about trust implies that management style may need to change.
- The speaker presents team culture as irrelevant to remote work.

Gap Fill prefers advanced vocabulary, discourse-level language, and complex expressions:

- The speaker highlights a tension between individual ______ and team cohesion.
- Trust is presented as an underlying ______ in remote management.
- Informal conversations may contribute to long-term ______.

Word bank:

- autonomy
- assumption
- team cohesion
- underlying tension
- organizational culture

Discussion questions ask for interpretation and evaluation:

- What implicit assumption does the speaker make about trust?
- How does the contrast between focus and culture shape the argument?
- Which concern feels most nuanced, and which feels underdeveloped?
